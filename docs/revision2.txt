Admin Role & System-Wide Enhancements
• Admin Activity Logs Pagination: Applied pagination—displays 6 recent entries by
default with an Expand option to view full history. (Fixed)
• Placeholder Labels: Replaced placeholder account labels with standardized
default names (e.g., School Head, Admin). (Fixed)
• Profile Security: Mandatory manual input required for the "Current Password"
field during profile updates (autofill disabled). (Fixed)
• Sidebar Navigation: Moved the sidebar collapse/expand toggle button directly
into the navbar bar instead of the page body. Expanding the sidebar flips the
brand logo. (Fixed)
• User Guide Flowchart: Added an easy-to-follow PDF system guide with
explanations designed for non-technical users. (Fixed)
 Teacher Role Features & Fixes
• Academic Year: Dynamically synced to the real-time active school year. (Fixed)
• My Sections View: Removed the global "All Sections" view. Teachers will now
only see "My Sections" (the specific sections they are actively teaching). (Fixed)
• Subject & Document Filtering: The Document Management section now filters
files to display only those relevant to the teacher's designated subjects. (Fixed)
• Grade Change Requests: Added a field for common mistakes when requesting
grade modifications (optional). (Fixed)
• Upload Grades Template: Fixed formatting issues in the
downloadable/uploadable grade entry template. (Fixed)
• School Form (SF) Generation: Restricted SF generation exclusively to a teacher's
assigned students and sections. (Fixed)
• Recently Enrolled List (Student Management): Shows 6 recent students by
default, with an Expand button to view the rest. (Fixed)
• Graduated Students Status Updates: Graduated students can no longer be reenrolled; accounts automatically lock/archive to preserve historical section and
academic data. (Fixed)
 School Form Updates
• School Form 9 (SF9) Gender Selection: Converted the gender input on SFU to a
standardized dropdown menu. (Fixed)
