Summary
What the system should do once it follows the Strengthened SHS Curriculum: two tracks
(Academic and TechPro) instead of strands, five core subjects in Grade 11, electives
chosen per learner, and Work Immersion in Grade 12. The system goes live next school
year, when both Grade 11 and Grade 12 follow the new curriculum, so it does not need to
support the old strands or two curricula side by side.
Whatto build first(the minimum)
1. Both tracks on the SHS enrollment form. The system offers Academic and TechnicalProfessional (TechPro). The Track field replaces the strand picker for Grade 11 and
Grade 12.
2. Each track shows its electives. When the user clicks Academic or TechPro, the
clusters under that track appear, and each cluster lists the electives the school offers,
for the student to choose.
3. The five core subjects. Seed them for Grade 11 only and assign them automatically.
4. Electives as a simple list. For now, assume DSPMNHS offers every elective and NC II
specialization in the reference lists at the end, except the Maritime Transport cluster,
which is removed. Each elective has an on or off switch, so the Administrator can hide
any the school does not offer. The Registrar or Enrollment Committee picks the
electives per learner.
5. Chosen electives follow the learner everywhere. When a learner is given an elective,
that subject appears in the learner's Grade Management, so the elective's teacher can
encode grades, and it counts in the General Average. It also prints on the learner's
School Forms: SF9 and SF10 list the elective with its grades, and SF1 shows the track
and electives. TechPro electives are graded like any other subject, with quarterly
grades and the same 75 passing mark.
6. Field Experience and Work Immersion use numerical grades only. They are graded
like any other subject, and the system stores no hours for them.

7. Print track and electives on SF1, SF9, and SF10 for SHS learners.
Whatto skip for now
A screen to manage clusters, and cluster rules. Clusters appear only as fixed headings
on the elective list.
Storing Work Immersion and Field Experience hours (still in the limitations).
Support for the old strand curriculum, because the system goes live when all SHS
learners follow the new one.
The per-curriculum grading terms setting.
Migration tooling for existing learners, unless real Grade 11 records already exist.
Everything skipped goes into the manuscript's limitations or recommendations.
Enrollmentform behavior
The SHS Strand picker goes away. Rules marked (recommended) are our design choices,
not DepEd rules.
1. The SHS enrollment form for Mainstream/Regular and Open High School, Grades 11 and
12, offers both tracks, Academic and Technical-Professional (TechPro), as a required
Track field that replaces the SHS Strand picker.
2. When the user clicks Academic or TechPro, the clusters under that track appear, and
under each cluster the electives the school offers. The learner picks from that list.
Because DepEd lets learners choose across tracks, a Show electives from the other
track option keeps the other track's electives reachable.
3. (Recommended) Electives may be left empty at enrollment, like the Document
Checklist, so a missing choice never blocks enrollment. The Enrollment Committee
must fill them before confirming a section.
4. Grade 11: assign the five core subjects automatically; they are not selectable. Grade 12:
assign no core subjects, and assign Work Immersion automatically.
5. ALS SHS keeps its own learning strands and specialization, unchanged. Do not apply
tracks or clusters to ALS.
6. PWD learners stay auto-tagged SNED and may take any track.
7. Log every track or elective change in the Activity Log with the actor and the old and
new values.

Subjects and grade encoding
The big shift: electives are chosen per student, so a class roster for an elective is the
students who picked it, not a whole section. Core subjects stay section-wide.
Subject Management (Administrator)
Create SHS subjects by category: core, elective, Field Experience, Work Immersion.
Block saving a core subject for any grade other than 11.
Each elective has an on or off switch for the school year. An elective that is off does
not appear on the enrollment form. Electives sit under fixed cluster headings, and
there is no screen for managing clusters.
Teacher assignment and rosters
Assign a teacher to each elective offering. Build the roster from student_electives .
Core subjects keep the existing section roster.
The class list, grade encoding, Excel upload, and the at-risk badge must all read the
elective roster, or an elective teacher will see the wrong students.
The schedule and room modules need to accept an elective timeslot whose roster cuts
across sections.
Grades
General Average counts every graded SHS subject the learner has. Grade 11 has the
cores plus electives; Grade 12 has electives only. Passing mark (75 default) and
monitoring threshold (80 default) stayAdministrator settings.
Work Immersion and Field Experience are graded with numerical grades only, like any
other subject, and count in the General Average. The system stores no hours for them.
The number of grading periods stays at three quarters, as in the manuscript. Confirm
the term structure with DepEd.
Grade locking, grade correction requests, and the Activity Log work as they do now, for
electives too.

SHS sections group learners by grade level and, optionally, track. Learners in one
section take the same core subjects in Grade 11 but different electives.
The Carryover method must carry the section from Grade 11 to Grade 12, and copy the
learner's electives as a proposed list the Enrollment Committee can edit.
The Enrollment Committee still confirms every placement. Teachers still cannot place
students.
Promotion and completion
Keep the rule that a final grade under 75 means Retained, and apply it to electives too.
Confirm with DepEd how a failed elective affects promotion.
At-risk detection
The formula does not change: quarterly General Averages, least-squares trend,
computed on demand. Make sure it reads elective grades and uses only the current
school year's quarters, because the subjects behind the General Average differ
between Grade 11 and Grade 12.
School Forms and reports
The system generates SF1, SF5, SF9, and SF10 as PDF. For SHS learners they show the
track and electives instead of the strand.
Form Change for SHS learners
SF1 School Register Show track instead of strand in the SHS rows and
classification columns.
SF5 Report on Promotion Group SHS by track; include elective results in the
promotion check. It is still a class-level summary.
SF9 Report Card Grade 11 lists the five core subjects plus electives;
Grade 12 lists electives, Field Experience, and Work
Immersion, all with numerical grades. Keep the
customizable extra rows.
