TEACHER ROLE — Adviser Management
Add an Adviser Management module. This means the teacher only sees and manages the students
under their advisory class, nothing outside of that. (FIXED)
---
TEACHER ROLE — Grade Management Restrictions
A teacher can only encode grades for the subject they are assigned to handle. They can view other
subjects' grades but cannot edit them. Subject-level permission locks grade encoding to the
teacher's designated subject only. (NOT FIXED- IF TEACHER IS MATH TEACHER HE CAN ONLY EDIT
THE GRADES IN STUDENT’s MATH SUBJ)
---
TEACHER ROLE — Grade Editing Time Limit
Once grades are submitted, there is a time limit for making changes. After the deadline, the teacher
can no longer edit on their own. Any correction must go through the Registrar. (NOT FIXED)
PRINCIPAL ROLE — Export
The Principal should be able to export all school documents, not just view them. Export access
should cover school-wide reports and data. (NOT FIXED)
PRINCIPAL ROLE — Graphs
The Principal's dashboard should include graphs and visual analytics, not just numbers or tables.
Charts showing enrollment trends, grade distributions, at-risk counts, and similar data should be
visible at a glance. (FIXED)
